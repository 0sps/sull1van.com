# Chunk 01 research notes (ex001-ex037)

## Formula used

For externally loaded dynamic resistance exercises, the mapping uses the Epley estimate `w*(1+(r/30))`. The [Epley formula reference](https://www.1rmcalculator.org/formulas/epley) states that exact equation and recommends low-repetition, near-failure sets. NASM independently describes estimating 1RM from submaximal weight and repetitions and says this can be done for a squat, bench press, deadlift, or other exercise in its [one-rep-max calculator guidance](https://www.nasm.org/resources/one-rep-max-calculator). This is an estimated 1RM, not a recommendation to attempt a true maximum on isolation or machine exercises.

Strength Level's [generic calculator](https://strengthlevel.com/one-rep-max-calculator) explicitly supports calculating a 1RM for any lift. Exercise-specific pages establish that 1RM is meaningfully tracked for the families represented in this chunk:

- Presses: [incline dumbbell bench press](https://strengthlevel.com/strength-standards/incline-dumbbell-bench-press), [dumbbell bench press](https://strengthlevel.com/strength-standards/dumbbell-bench-press), and [dumbbell shoulder press](https://strengthlevel.com/strength-standards/dumbbell-shoulder-press). This covers ex001, ex005, ex017, and the machine chest-press family ex034.
- Squat, hinge, and unilateral leg work: [Romanian deadlift and Bulgarian split squat](https://strengthlevel.com/strength-standards/romanian-deadlift-vs-bulgarian-split-squat/lb), plus the general strength-standards catalog's squat and leg-press entries. This covers ex006, ex013, ex025, and ex028.
- Cable, machine, and isolation work: [cable lateral raise](https://strengthlevel.com/strength-standards/cable-lateral-raise), [dumbbell lateral raise](https://strengthlevel.com/strength-standards/dumbbell-lateral-raise), [cable crunch](https://strengthlevel.com/strength-standards/cable-crunch/lb), [seated leg curl](https://strengthlevel.com/strength-standards/seated-leg-curl/lb), [machine chest fly](https://strengthlevel.com/strength-standards/machine-chest-fly), [triceps pushdown](https://strengthlevel.com/strength-standards/tricep-pushdown/lb), [seated calf raise](https://strengthlevel.com/strength-standards/seated-calf-raise/lb), and [machine calf raise](https://strengthlevel.com/strength-standards/machine-calf-raise/kg). These substantiate the same loaded-movement treatment for ex002, ex004, ex007-ex012, ex014-ex015, ex018-ex024, ex026, and ex030-ex037.

## `NONE` decisions

- `ex003` Pull Up: bodyweight movement, and the user explicitly directed that pull-ups be `NONE` even though weighted variants exist.
- `ex016` Incline Back Extension: the unqualified bodyweight version is conventionally tracked by repetitions; the [back-extension standards](https://strengthlevel.com/strength-standards/back-extension/lb) report rep counts rather than loaded 1RM.
- `ex027` Step Up: the unqualified entry specifies no external load or equipment and is treated as the bodyweight exercise. A separately named dumbbell/barbell step-up could be classified as externally loaded.
- `ex029` Bicycle Crunch: bodyweight abdominal repetition exercise with no external load.

## Scope and caveats

Machine-stack numbers are machine-specific, and unilateral/dumbbell records must keep the same weight convention over time (per implement versus combined load). Epley estimates are most defensible for hard sets in a low rep range; they become less reliable as repetitions increase.
