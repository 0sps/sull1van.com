# Chunk 07 1RM research (ex223-ex259)

## Formula selected

For fixed-load, repetition-based resistance lifts in this chunk, the selected equation is the Epley formula, `w*(1+(r/30))`. RepMax publishes that exact equation and describes its calculator as applying to any lift. Calculator.net independently lists the same Epley equation as a common 1RM estimation method. Use low-repetition, near-maximal sets because formula accuracy declines as reps rise.

- https://repmax.co.uk/
- https://www.calculator.net/one-rep-max-calculator.html
- https://www.nasm.org/resources/one-rep-max-calculator

## NONE classifications

- Cardio machines: ex223 Elliptical, ex227 Stair Stepper.
- Bodyweight, suspension, or explicit pull-up/chin-up variants: ex228 Assisted Pull Up, ex229 TRX Chest Press, ex231 Superman with Scaption, ex247 Assisted Chin Up, ex252 Toes to Bar, ex253 Tuck Crunch, ex254 Jackknife Sit-Up, ex257 TRX Resisted Rotation 1.
- Isometric/timed: ex225 Anterior Isometric Neck, ex230 Superman Hold.
- Ballistic jump or slam without a meaningful maximal single-load convention: ex224 Medicine Ball Slam, ex245 Lateral Box Jump.
- Variable band resistance, where `w` is not a stable comparable load: ex240 Loop Band Standing Decline Chest Press, ex246 Mini Loop Band Lateral Walk.

## Blank classification

- ex236 Face Down Plate Neck Resistance is externally loadable and repetition-based, but no reliable lift-specific or clearly validated neck-resistance 1RM formula was found. It is therefore left blank rather than borrowing a formula without adequate support.
