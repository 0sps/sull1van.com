# Chunk 08 1RM research (ex260-ex296)

## Formula choice

All supported entries use the Epley formula, `w*(1+(r/30))`. An online Epley calculator gives that exact expression and describes it as exercise-independent for barbell and machine lifts: https://www.thestandardengine.com/health/epley-one-rep-max-calculator. Strength Level also provides a reps-based 1RM calculator for any lift and documents use of the Epley equation for higher-repetition inputs: https://strengthlevel.com/one-rep-max-calculator and https://strengthlevel.com/faq.

## Exercise-specific support

- **ex260 EZ-Bar Skullcrusher; ex273 Incline Barbell Skull Crusher:** the lying triceps extension family has published 1RM standards: https://strengthlevel.com/strength-standards/lying-tricep-extension/lb.
- **ex263 Palms-Down Barbell Wrist Curl:** the corresponding reverse wrist curl has published 1RM standards: https://strengthlevel.com/strength-standards/reverse-wrist-curl/lb.

- **ex270 Pause Barbell Incline Bench Press:** incline bench has named 1RM standards and a named calculator that offers Epley: https://strengthlevel.com/strength-standards/incline-bench-press/lb and https://www.strengthmath.com/calculators/incline-bench-1rm-calculator.
- **ex274 Hip Thrust:** named-lift 1RM standards: https://strengthlevel.com/strength-standards/hip-thrust/lb.
- **ex276 Muscle Snatch:** named-lift 1RM standards: https://strengthlevel.com/strength-standards/muscle-snatch.
- **ex277 Pause Bench Press:** named-lift 1RM standards: https://strengthlevel.com/strength-standards/paused-bench-press.
- **ex280 V-Bar Pulldown:** the lat-pulldown family is explicitly supported by Strength Level's exercise selector and general reps-based calculator: https://strengthlevel.com/strength-standards/pause-squat and https://strengthlevel.com/one-rep-max-calculator.
- **ex285 Snatch:** named-lift 1RM standards: https://strengthlevel.com/strength-standards/snatch.
- **ex288 Kettlebell Overhead Press:** treated as the same weighted overhead-press family as dumbbell shoulder press, for which 1RM standards are published: https://strengthlevel.com/strength-standards/dumbbell-shoulder-press.
- **ex289 Floor Press:** named-lift 1RM standards: https://strengthlevel.com/strength-standards/floor-press.
- **ex290 Smith Machine Stiff-Legged Deadlift:** stiff-leg deadlift has named 1RM standards, and the formula source explicitly covers machine lifts: https://strengthlevel.com/strength-standards/stiff-leg-deadlift/lb.
- **ex292 Hang Power Clean:** named-lift 1RM standards: https://strengthlevel.com/strength-standards/hang-power-clean/lb.
- **ex293 Pause Back Squat:** named-lift 1RM standards: https://strengthlevel.com/strength-standards/pause-squat.
- **ex294 Smith Machine Bench Press:** named-lift 1RM standards: https://strengthlevel.com/strength-standards/smith-machine-bench-press.

## Blank classifications

- **ex261 Balance Trainer Cable Crunch With Side Bends:** externally loaded, but this unstable combination movement lacks reliable named-lift formula support.
- **ex264 Handle Band Pec Fly:** band resistance does not provide a standardized `w` input, and no reliable named formula was found.
- **ex275 Single Leg Kickback:** equipment/load is unspecified and the name may describe either bodyweight or cable resistance, so no formula was assigned.
- **ex279 Walking Lunge:** load and distance/rep convention are unspecified; no reliable formula for this exact movement was found.
- **ex291 Renegade Row:** externally loaded, but its alternating plank/row structure lacks reliable named-lift formula support.

## `NONE` classifications

ex262, ex266-ex269, ex271-ex272, ex278, ex281-ex284, ex286-ex287, and ex295-ex296 are bodyweight, suspension, timed/isometric, conditioning, or push-up movements without a meaningful external-weight 1RM under the requested rules.
