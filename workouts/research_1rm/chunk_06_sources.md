# Chunk 06 research: ex186-ex222

Formula used where substantiated: `w*(1+(r/30))`, the Epley equation. The formula is published explicitly as `1RM = Weight × (1 + Reps ÷ 30)` by Strength Level Calculator, which says it works for all lifts and specifically names Zottman curls. Strength Level's FAQ independently confirms that its exercise standards derive from logged lifts and that its 1RM calculator uses Epley at higher rep counts (with Brzycki at lower counts). The exercise-specific sources below establish that the named movement, or its direct movement family, is tracked as a 1RM or estimated from a weight-and-repetition set.

- Epley equation and general applicability: https://strengthlevelcalculator.com/
- Independent formula reference: https://strengthlevel.com/faq

## Exercises assigned the Epley formula

- **ex192 Shotgun Row:** AtletIQ prescribes Shotgun Row loads as percentages of its 1RM and accepts working weight plus maximum reps: https://atletiq.com/en/exercises/1121
- **ex193 Smith Machine Incline Bench Press:** Fitbod provides a named Weight & 1 Rep Max Calculator; StrengthMath also says its incline estimator accepts Smith-machine incline sets: https://fitbod.me/exercises/smith-machine-incline-bench-press and https://www.strengthmath.com/calculators/incline-bench-1rm-calculator
- **ex194 Dumbbell Decline Fly:** the named exercise page explicitly directs externally weighted movements to a 1RM calculator: https://www.drfitology.com/exercises/chest/dumbbell-decline-fly
- **ex197 Smith Machine Shrug:** Strength Level publishes named one-rep-max standards, and Dr. Fitology explicitly links this exercise to 1RM estimation: https://strengthlevel.pl/standardy-sily/szrugsy-na-maszynie-smitha/lb and https://www.drfitology.com/exercises/traps/smith-machine-shrug
- **ex198 Cable Shoulder Press:** the named standards page tells users to estimate this lift's 1RM from a rep set: https://fitnessvolt.com/strength-standards/cable-shoulder-press/
- **ex201 Stiff-Legged Barbell Good Morning:** the direct barbell-good-morning family calculator estimates 1RM from weight and reps; its worked 185 lb x 5 result is about 216 lb, matching Epley: https://endura.coach/barbell-good-morning-strength-standards-calculator/
- **ex204 Cable Wrist Curl:** the seated cable wrist curl page explicitly recommends its 1RM calculator for the exercise: https://www.drfitology.com/exercises/forearms/seated-cable-wrist-curl
- **ex206 Zottman Curl:** the Epley source explicitly lists Zottman curl among supported lifts; a separate named standards page asks for a Zottman Curl 1RM: https://strengthlevelcalculator.com/ and https://fitnessvolt.com/strength-standards/zottman-curl/
- **ex207 Barbell Bicep Drag Curl:** the named calculator estimates 1RM from working weight and reps: https://endura.coach/barbell-drag-curl-strength-standards-calculator/
- **ex212 Dumbbell Goblet Squat:** a worked goblet-squat example explicitly applies Epley to weight and reps; a separate standards page expresses Goblet Squat values as 1RM: https://medium.com/@ghayyoorphilosophy/one-rep-max-formula-explained-for-gym-beginners-dc48d8c340c4 and https://fitnessvolt.com/strength-standards/goblet-squat/
- **ex216 Smith Machine Calf Raise:** the named standards calculator accepts a repeatable 3-5-rep training weight for a max estimate: https://endura.coach/smith-machine-calf-raise-strength-standards-calculator/
- **ex217 Dumbbell Kickbacks:** WorkoutPath lists Tricep Kickback among exercises for its weight-and-reps 1RM calculator: https://workoutpath.fit/
- **ex219 Behind the Back Cable Lateral Raise:** Fitbod provides a named Weight & 1 Rep Max Calculator based on logged sets: https://fitbod.me/exercises/behind-the-back-cable-lateral-raise
