# Chunk 09 1RM research (ex297-ex331)

## Formula used

`w*(1+(r/30))` is the Epley equation. EVOX states the equation explicitly and says its calculator applies it to supported exercises including Power Snatch, Hang Snatch, Front Squat, Thruster, Kettlebell Swing, and related strength lifts: https://www.getevox.fit/rm-calculator

- **ex305 Single Arm Kettlebell Swing**: Epley applied from the supported Kettlebell Swing family. The single-arm variation retains a fixed external kettlebell load and a meaningful rep maximum.
- **ex307 Box Front Squat**: Epley applied from the supported Front Squat family. The box changes the endpoint but this remains a fixed-load front squat.
- **ex310 Power Snatch**: EVOX explicitly lists Power Snatch among lifts supported by its Epley 1RM calculator.
- **ex311 Snatch Deadlift**: FitnessVolt has a lift-specific Snatch Deadlift page that directs users to estimate its 1RM from any rep set; its calculator identifies Epley as one of its established weight-and-reps formulas and says these formulas work for any barbell lift: https://fitnessvolt.com/strength-standards/snatch-deadlift/ and https://fitnessvolt.com/one-rep-max-calculator/
- **ex315 Power Clean Thruster**: Epley applied from the supported Thruster family. A completed rep includes the power clean setup and thruster, but the scored strength movement remains a fixed-load thruster.
- **ex317 Box Squat**: FitnessVolt's Box Squat page explicitly directs users to estimate the lift's 1RM from a rep set; Epley is one of the formulas identified by its linked calculator. EVOX also supports the Back Squat family with Epley: https://fitnessvolt.com/strength-standards/box-squat/ and https://fitnessvolt.com/one-rep-max-calculator/
- **ex323 Hang Snatch**: EVOX explicitly lists Hang Snatch among lifts supported by its Epley 1RM calculator.
- **ex325 Kettlebell Swing**: EVOX explicitly lists Kettlebell Swing among lifts supported by its Epley 1RM calculator.

## NONE classifications

`NONE` is used for elastic-band movements (ex297, ex301, ex322, ex328, ex331), push-up/bodyweight movements (ex302, ex308, ex309, ex318, ex319, ex326, ex330), and suspension-trainer/bodyweight movements (ex303, ex312, ex314). These do not have a consistent external weight input suitable for a weight-and-reps 1RM equation.

## Blank classifications

The remaining externally loaded movements were left blank because no sufficiently reliable, lift-specific or clearly applicable-family evidence for an exact weight-and-reps formula was found: ex298, ex299, ex300, ex304, ex306, ex313, ex316, ex320, ex321, ex324, ex327, and ex329.
